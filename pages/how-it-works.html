Assistant onboarding

From first call to active support, we keep the process practical and clear.

Inspired by proven assistant-placement workflows, URSVA maps your needs, recommends the right support path, and prepares your assistant to work inside your actual tools and routines. No client dashboard required.

Step 01

Book a consultation

Start with a short call so we can understand your workload, your bottlenecks, and the kind of support that would make the fastest difference.

Step 02

Tell us what you need

We define the role, hours, communication style, tools, recurring tasks, and any client-facing responsibilities your assistant may handle.

Step 03

Review the support plan

URSVA recommends the best support setup, skill priorities, and operating rhythm before work begins, so expectations are clear on both sides.

Step 04

Meet and confirm the fit

When assistant support is part of the plan, you review the proposed fit, confirm responsibilities, and align on start date, schedule, and access.

Step 05

Onboard tools and workflows

We help organize the handoff: inboxes, calendars, trackers, SOPs, communication channels, permissions, and the first-priority task list.

Step 06

Start work and improve

Your assistant begins supporting day-to-day work while URSVA keeps an eye on clarity, consistency, and process improvements as needs evolve.
